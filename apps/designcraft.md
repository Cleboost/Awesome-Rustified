## Summary

DesignCraft targets InDesign-like publishing: threaded text frames, parent pages, folios, and a Rust-native paragraph composer for magazines, reports, and multi-page layouts.

## Stack

- [craft-libs](https://github.com/storytold/craft-libs)

## Why Rust

Layout engine, typography, and UI share one codebase for responsive editing on large documents.
