## Summary

CADCraft is computer-aided design and drafting in pure Rust: model space, paper space viewports, MTEXT, tables, dimensions, hatching, and DXF/DWG-oriented workflows — clean-room from AutoCAD-style behavior.

## Stack

- [craft-libs](https://github.com/storytold/craft-libs)

## Why Rust

CAD geometry, IO, and interaction in one native app instead of a legacy plugin host.
