## Summary

WordCraft reimplements a Word-like writing experience in Rust: ribbon UI, styles, track changes, comments, references, tables of contents, and page layout — clean-room, open source.

## Stack

- [craft-libs](https://github.com/storytold/craft-libs)

## Why Rust

Document model, layout, and UI in one stack without an Office webview or Electron wrapper.
