## Summary

ArtCraft Launcher is the hub for the Craft App Suite: one Rust app to install, update, and open PhotoCraft, LightCraft, FilmCraft, DesignCraft, VectorCraft, PdfCraft, EffectCraft, WordCraft, DeckCraft, GridCraft, CADCraft, SoundCraft, and future Craft apps — right build for your OS.

## Stack

- Pure Rust launcher; downloads release artifacts for each Craft app
- Shares branding and packaging patterns with [craft-libs](https://github.com/storytold/craft-libs)

## Why Rust

Single lightweight installer/updater instead of a separate Electron “store” per product.
