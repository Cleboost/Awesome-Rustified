## Summary

GridCraft is a clean-room spreadsheet: formulas, formatting, conditional rules, charts, and sample workbooks — native Rust, Excel-workflow familiar.

## Stack

- [craft-libs](https://github.com/storytold/craft-libs)

## Why Rust

Recalc, rendering, and interaction stay in-process for large sheets without a browser-based grid host.
