## Summary

PdfCraft is a PDF workbench: forms, optional content layers, comments, attachments, and page rendering — implemented from ISO 32000 and black-box observation, not from proprietary Adobe code.

## Stack

- [craft-libs](https://github.com/storytold/craft-libs)
- Clean-room PDF engine in-repo

## Why Rust

Parsing, rendering, and UI share one memory-safe codebase suitable for large documents.
